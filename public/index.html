Every vehicle includes a 2.4G wireless parent remote controller with up to 30m range. Parents can override steering and press emergency brake instantly.
How long does the battery last on a single charge? ▼
Our batteries provide 60 to 90 minutes of continuous trail driving per charge depending on rider weight and terrain.

All vehicles include a 1-Year Manufacturer Warranty covering motors, battery, motherboard electronics, and parent remote control.
